# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want AI SOC Copilot to be a welcoming, technical, and respectful open-source project. Contributors should communicate professionally, assume good intent, and focus feedback on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep technical discussions focused on improving the project.
- Protect privacy and do not share sensitive incident data or credentials.
- Follow responsible security disclosure practices.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and sharing private or sensitive information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Security issues should be reported according to `SECURITY.md` rather than disclosed publicly.
